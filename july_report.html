Decision drawers

Approve Bonus rules

Only Pending bonuses can be approved. Approval stamps status, date, user, and timestamp. No approval reason is required in Phase 1. A simple confirmation dialog is required. Approve does not post to payroll.

Status Details display rules

Status Details appears for Rejected or Void bonuses. Reject reason appears only for Rejected. Void details appear only for Void. Pending and Approved records keep the section hidden.

Excel/export guardrail

Excel exports remain output artifacts. FileMaker remains the source record. Future wording should avoid implying that exported spreadsheets become the official truth.

Phase 2 full-detail export/report

Full audit/status detail should move to a detailed export/report instead of overloading daily working layouts. This protects usability while preserving evidence and review detail.
