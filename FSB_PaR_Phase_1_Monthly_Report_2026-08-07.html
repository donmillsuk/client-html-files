FSB_PaR Phase 1 Status Update — 7 August 2026 Coverage: progress since the 30 June report. Completed Bonus controls: • Approve Bonus is built and live-tested. It accepts only saved Pending records, validates required details, stamps approval information and locks the record. • Reject, Void and Mark Posted are built and tested as controlled workflow actions. • Void is allowed only from Pending or Approved; Posted records cannot be voided directly. • Locked Bonus details are protected from casual editing, with clear user feedback. • Status Details shows the relevant Reject or Void information without crowding the normal working screen. • Bonus and adjustment classification is ready with Bonus, Reimbursement, Correction, Other Payment and Cell Phone. Completed pay-rate integrity work: • FileMaker now prevents more than one Active pay-rate row for the same employee and blocks the save if that safety check cannot complete. • OvertimeRate and DoubletimeRate are preserved on each pay-rate row at 1.5 and 2 times the base rate. • The pay-rate review screen now includes the employee name, making review practical. • Historical rates remain in place; changes are handled through Supersede or Void rather than rewriting history. Pay Packet direction now approved: • Phase 1 produces a human-readable on-screen and printable packet for manual ADP entry. It does not create an ADP upload file. • The packet will show regular or salary compensation, overtime, doubletime, Vacation, Personal, Sick, Holiday, bonuses, corrections, non-taxable reimbursements and contractor compensation. • Vacation and Personal will be recorded separately instead of a single combined PTO choice. • Unapproved or incomplete items are excluded from totals, displayed as warnings and block Ready for Payroll or Posting. • Packet rows may be regenerated while a pay period is Open or Ready for Payroll; once Posted, the packet becomes a preserved snapshot. • Salaried employees keep an hourly equivalent rate in FileMaker. ADP shows normal salary as Regular compensation, while leave hours are informational with no additional pay. Current position: The evidence and design blockers have been cleared. The next build work is the small Vacation/Personal time-entry change, followed by the Pay Packet helper table, report layout, generation workflow and Cindy-first operational testing. Source-of-truth rule: FileMaker remains the protected payroll source. Reports and spreadsheets are outputs for review and sharing.
Technical Decision Summary — 7 August 2026 Bonus lifecycle now implemented: • Statuses: Pending, Approved, Rejected, Posted and Void. • SCR_Approve_Bonus, SCR_MarkPosted_Bonus and SCR_Void_Bonus were generated through the controlled FMXML workflow, sandbox-tested and promoted to the live file. • SCR_SaveRecord_Bonuses has a tested lock guard. • From Approved, the only allowed next states are Posted or Void. • Posting sets IsPostedToPayroll; approval does not. • AdjustmentType values: Bonus, Reimbursement, Correction, Other Payment, Cell Phone. Pay-rate integrity now implemented: • TBL_EmployeePayRates::Status has fail-closed validation enforcing one Active rate per employee. • OvertimeRate auto-enters PayRate × 1.5. • DoubletimeRate auto-enters PayRate × 2. • Rate history is preserved through SCR_SupersedeRate and SCR_VoidRate. • LYT_EmployeePayRates_Review includes TBL_Employees::EmployeeName. Approved time-entry change: • Rename TBL_TimeEntries::HoursPTO to HoursPaidLeave. • VL_TimeEntryType becomes ProjectWork, CompanyWork, Vacation, Personal, Holiday, Sick and Other. • EntryType identifies the leave category; one HoursPaidLeave field holds the hours. Approved Pay Packet helper design: • Table: TBL_PayrollPacketRows; layout: LYT_PayrollPacket. • One row per payroll component, with source tracking and preserved employee/company/pay-type snapshots. • Line fields hold LineCode, description, hours, rate, amount, tax treatment, disposition, warning and sort order. • Calculations and summaries provide included hours, taxable/non-taxable amounts and warning counts. • Open and Ready packets can regenerate. Posted packet rows are preserved and cannot be overwritten. Phase boundary: • Phase 1 remains manual ADP entry from a reviewed packet. • ADP integration, receipts, persistent data-store ideas and fuller audit reporting remain Phase 2 unless they materially simplify the core delivery.

Decision drawers

Completed Bonus lifecycle

Pending records remain editable. Approved, Rejected, Posted and Void records are locked. Approve validates and stamps approval. Reject and Void require controlled reasons. Mark Posted is separate from approval and is the only action that sets the payroll-post flag.

Pay-rate integrity repair

Each employee may have only one Active pay-rate row. The duplicate check fails closed, historical rows are preserved, and overtime/doubletime values are stored from the base rate. The review screen now includes the employee name.

Manual ADP Pay Packet

Phase 1 creates a readable on-screen and printable packet for manual ADP entry. It includes all approved payroll components and clearly separates excluded, unapproved or incomplete items. Posted packet rows become a preserved snapshot.

Vacation and Personal split

Vacation and Personal are recorded separately, alongside Holiday and Sick. One paid-leave hours field is retained; the entry type identifies which kind of leave the hours represent.

Phase 2 boundary

ADP integration, receipt features, persistent data-store ideas and fuller audit/report refinements stay out of Phase 1 unless they clearly simplify delivery of the core system.
